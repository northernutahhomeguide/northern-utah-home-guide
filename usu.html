Utah State University

Moving to Logan for Utah State University

For new faculty and staff, graduate students and parents: getting around, housing, nearby neighborhoods and the academic calendar.

USU at a glance

  • Fall 2025 total statewide enrollment: 29,831 (up 3.2% from 28,900 the prior year).
  • Logan campus fall 2025: 18,314 undergraduate and 2,257 graduate students (about 20,571 combined).
  • USU is a Carnegie R1 (very high research activity) university founded March 8, 1888; the Logan campus covers 600 acres.
  • Total USU workforce (fall 2025): 12,437, including about 1,200 faculty and 1,367 at the Space Dynamics Lab.
  • Logan is about 82 miles north of Salt Lake City; 2020 census population 52,778.

Sources: [1] [2] [3]

Getting around

  • Aggie Shuttle is free on its regular routes: five fixed routes Mon-Fri 7 a.m.-5 p.m. plus an evening route 5-10 p.m.; no summer service. Real-time tracking via Passio GO!.
  • Cache Valley Transit District rebranded as Connect Public Transit in 2024; buses remain zero fare.
  • USU HR notes Connect Public Transit Route 1 serves campus, Merrill-Cazier Library and Aggie Village.
  • Aggie Blue Bikes offers free daily and 3-month bike check-outs to students.
  • Logan campus parking requires a USU permit, bought through the USU Parking Portal; lots use license plate recognition and virtual permits. 2026-27 student permits opened July 1 (residents) and July 8 (commuters).
  • New employees buy a parking permit by calling 435-797-3414 with plate number, vehicle make/model/year and employee type.

Sources: [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

New faculty and staff

  • Benefit-eligible employees attend New Blue Employee Orientation (in person on the Logan campus, or live on Zoom). HR: hr@usu.edu, (435) 797-0122.
  • USU Policy 3010 allows hiring departments to offer discretionary relocation assistance to faculty and exempt hires; moving payments are taxable income to the employee.

Sources: [1] [2]

Housing for students

  • On-campus single student options include the Living Learning Community, Blue Square Apartments, Canyon Crest Suites, Central Suites, Richards, Bullen, Merrill and Ridge Point halls, and the Student Living Center halls.
  • Family housing still exists: Aggie Village (1, 2 and 3 bedroom), Townhomes and West Stadium Villa. Applicants must be USU students enrolled in at least 6 credits.
  • 2027-28 housing contracts for new students open December 1, 2026; family housing application requests open November 4.

Sources: [1] [2]

Neighborhoods and towns near campus

  • The Island is a Logan neighborhood between First Dam and the two branches of the Logan River, near the mouth of Logan Canyon; its north end sits across from the USU campus.
  • North Logan is a suburb adjoining Logan (2020 population 10,986) and is home to Green Canyon High School (Cache County School District).
  • Providence lies about 2.5 miles south of Logan (2020 population 8,218).
  • River Heights is a small Cache County city (2020 population 2,144) in the Logan metro area.
  • Most of Logan is served by Logan City School District; North Logan, Providence, River Heights and other Cache County communities are served by Cache County School District.

Sources: [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Calendar and campus life

  • Fall 2026: classes began Mon., Aug. 31; fall break Fri., Oct. 9; Thanksgiving Nov. 25-27; finals Dec. 14-18. Spring 2027 classes begin Jan. 11; Spring 2027 commencement May 5-7.
  • Utah State officially joined the Pac-12 on July 1, 2026 (2026-27 athletic year), after about a decade in the Mountain West.
  • Football plays at Merlin Olsen Field at Maverik Stadium, seating capacity 25,513, opened 1968.
  • Aggie Ice Cream dates to 1888; its parlor is in the Nutrition and Food Sciences building, 750 North 1200 East, with a second location at Blue Square (opened Sept. 2023). Signature flavor: Aggie Blue Mint.

Sources: [1] [2] [3] [4]

Schools and health care near campus

  • Edith Bowen Laboratory School is a K-6 public charter school on the USU campus (chartered in 2006 through Logan City School District); admission is by random selection.
  • Intermountain Logan Regional Hospital: 500 East 1400 North, Logan, UT 84341; (435) 716-1000.

Sources: [1] [2]

Rentals and investor notes

  • Logan requires a landlord license for anyone with one or more rental dwellings in city limits (Logan Municipal Code Chapter 5.17).
  • Logan's occupancy limit is one family, or no more than three unrelated individuals in all zones except the Campus Residential zone; landlords must disclose occupancy and zoning information to tenants (Sec. 5.17.095).
  • The Campus Residential zone near USU allows more unrelated residents; confirm current limits with Logan City before buying a rental.

Sources: [1] [2]
