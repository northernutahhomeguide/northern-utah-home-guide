Living in Garden City

Garden City sits on the western shore of Bear Lake at the east end of the Logan Canyon Scenic Byway (US-89), at about 6,200 feet elevation. The year-round population is small, and the housing stock includes many cabins, condos, and lakefront or hillside second homes and vacation rentals. Bear Lake State Park Marina, Heritage Park, and the Pickleville Playhouse are in or near town.

What's new in Garden City
  • August 2026
    New Bear Lake marina ahead of schedule
    The state's new marina just north of the current Bear Lake State Park Marina will more than double the boat slips and add launch lanes and deeper water. A warm winter put construction ahead of schedule; more than 80% of the digging was done by August, a partial opening is possible around fall 2027 and full opening is expected by May 2028. Source

Before you buy in Garden City: good to know4
  • Short-term rentals (30 days or less) require a town Short-Term Rental License Registration before advertising or renting; fee listed at $1,200 ($960 with a property management company), one license per unit, and the registration number must appear in every listing.
  • Main route to Cache Valley is US-89 through Logan Canyon, which can close temporarily in winter for storms or avalanche activity.
  • Elevation is about 6,200 ft; expect cold, snowy winters.
  • Town Public Works handles water; the town has a published Water Conservation Plan.
