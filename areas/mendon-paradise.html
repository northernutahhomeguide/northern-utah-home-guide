Mendon

Population ≈ 1,340 (2020 Census)Cache County School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesEventsGood to knowCommute

Mendon is a small city of about 1.2 square miles on the west side of Cache Valley at the base of the Wellsville Mountains, settled in 1859 and incorporated in 1870. City offices are in the restored 1915 Mendon Station railroad depot, and nine Mendon sites are on the National Register of Historic Places. Housing is a mix of older homes and newer construction with farmland on the town's edges.

Paradise

Population ≈ 970 (2020 Census)Cache County School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesGood to knowCommute

Paradise is a small town in the southern end of Cache Valley, settled in 1860 and sitting at about 4,950 feet with mountain views. The town covers roughly 1.4 square miles, with homes on in-town and rural lots surrounded by farmland. A Daughters of Utah Pioneers museum is located in town.
