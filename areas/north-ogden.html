Living in North Ogden

Population ≈ 20,900 (2020 Census)Weber School District

North Ogden sits at the base of Ben Lomond Peak about three miles north of Ogden on SR-235. Settled in 1850 and incorporated in 1934, it is mostly residential subdivisions with neighborhoods along the foothills. A large flag hangs above Coldwater Canyon each year to honor Mayor Brent R. Taylor and U.S. veterans.
