North Ogden sits at the base of Ben Lomond Peak about three miles north of Ogden on SR-235. Settled in 1850 and incorporated in 1934, it is mostly residential subdivisions with neighborhoods along the foothills. A large flag hangs above Coldwater Canyon each year to honor Mayor Brent R. Taylor and U.S. veterans.

Work & major employers

Federal government and defense (Hill AFB, IRS), aerospace and advanced manufacturing, education and health care.

Major employers in the area
  • Hill Air Force Base, just south, in Davis County: 10,000–14,999 workers; about 27,000 personnel including contractors
  • IRS Ogden campus, Ogden: Federal tax processing; sought 300+ new hires in fall 2025
  • Northrop Grumman, Roy: Aerospace and defense; broke ground on a sixth Roy building in July 2026
  • Weber School District, countywide: 4,000–4,999 employees
  • Weber State University, Ogden: 3,000–3,999 employees
  • McKay-Dee Hospital (Intermountain Health), Ogden: Regional hospital
  • Autoliv, Ogden: Automotive safety products
  • Williams International, Ogden: Jet engines; announced 316 new jobs in 2025
  • America First Credit Union, Ogden area: Credit union headquarters
  • Fresenius, Ogden: Medical products manufacturing
Recent job news
  • July 2026: Northrop Grumman broke ground on a sixth building at its Roy Innovation Center.
  • Sept. 2025: After 2025 staffing cuts, the IRS Ogden campus began hiring 300+ workers.

Weber County unemployment rate: 3.7% (August 2026, not seasonally adjusted). Source. Employee counts from the employers and the Utah Department of Workforce Services where available.

Winter
  • Three ski areas within a short drive: Snowbasin, Powder Mountain and Nordic Valley.
  • January averages 38°F highs and 22°F lows, a few degrees milder than Cache Valley.
  • Weber County is in the state's winter no-burn program, usually Nov. 1–Mar. 1.
Spring
  • April averages 62°F highs and 39°F lows; May is the wettest month.
  • FrontRunner commuter rail runs from Ogden and Roy to Salt Lake City and Provo year-round, Monday through Saturday.
Summer
  • Hot, dry summers: July averages 92°F highs.
  • Ogden Pioneer Days rodeo week in late July (July 18–24 in 2026).
  • Snowbasin's summer season runs from Father's Day weekend to early October, with about 25 miles of trails.
Fall
  • The Ogden Canyon and Pineview loop is a favorite fall-color drive, peaking late September to early October.

Before you buy in North Ogden: good to know3
  • The city's utility services include fiber installation, plus sewer, storm water and solid/green waste.
  • The city charges a Transportation Utility Fee (TUF) for streets.
  • Foothill location gives direct access to Ben Lomond-area trails.
