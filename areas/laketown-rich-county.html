Laketown

Population ≈ 300 (2020 Census)Rich School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Laketown is a small town near the south end of Bear Lake, close to Rendezvous Beach and the junction of SR-30 and the east-shore Cisco Road. It is home to North Rich Elementary and Rich Middle School. Properties range from in-town homes to larger rural and ranch parcels, with lake recreation a few minutes away.

Randolph

Population ≈ 470 (2020 Census)Rich School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Randolph is the Rich County seat, a small ranching town in the Bear River valley south of Bear Lake. The county courthouse, county library, senior center, fairgrounds, Rich High School, and South Rich Elementary are in town. Housing is mostly single-family homes on in-town lots plus surrounding agricultural and ranch land.

Before you buy in Randolph: good to know4
  • Town has an adopted short-term rental ordinance and application.
  • Town water comes from local wells (Field St. Well, Park Well) and Randolph Spring; Consumer Confidence Reports are posted on the town site.
  • Climate is long and cold with heavy snow (about 70 inches per year on average).
  • Planning & zoning, subdivision, and lot-split ordinances are posted on the town website.

Woodruff

Population ≈ 170 (2020 Census)Rich School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Woodruff is a small ranching town in southern Rich County at about 6,300 feet elevation, settled in 1870 and incorporated in 1933. The town is surrounded by agricultural land, and housing is mostly rural single-family homes.
