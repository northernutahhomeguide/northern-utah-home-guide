Living in River Heights

Population ≈ 2,100 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

River Heights is a small city on the bench directly southeast of Logan and north of Providence, at about 4,570 feet elevation. Settled in 1882 and incorporated in 1934, it is a primarily residential community with about 660 households. City offices are at 520 South 500 East.

Good to know before you buy in River Heights3
  • City council meets the 1st and 3rd Tuesdays; city office hours are limited (Mon-Thu, 9:30 a.m.-2:30 p.m.).
  • City handles drinking water, sewer and storm water; see riverheights.gov for utility rates.
  • Cache County School District reconfigured for 2026-27 (6th grade moved to 6-8 middle schools; new Juniper Ridge MS in Hyde Park and Maple View MS in Nibley). Confirm school assignments for a specific address on the district's boundary map.
