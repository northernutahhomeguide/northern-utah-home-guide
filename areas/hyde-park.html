Living in Hyde Park

Population ≈ 5,200 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

Hyde Park lies between North Logan and Smithfield, about five miles north of Logan, covering roughly 4.4 square miles at about 4,650 feet elevation. Founded in 1860, it is largely residential. The John E. Lee House is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and the city is home to the new Juniper Ridge Middle School and Old Ephraim Elementary (opened 2026).
