Living in West Haven

Population ≈ 16,700 (2020 Census)Weber School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesEventsGood to knowCommute

West Haven lies southwest of Ogden where the Weber and Ogden rivers meet. It was incorporated in 1991 from the communities of Kanesville and Wilson. One of Weber County's fastest-growing cities, it went from about 10,300 residents in 2010 to 16,700 in 2020, mostly through new subdivision development.
