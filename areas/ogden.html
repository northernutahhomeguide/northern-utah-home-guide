Living in Ogden

Population ≈ 87,300 (2020 Census)Ogden School District

Ogden is the Weber County seat and Utah's eighth-largest city, set at the base of the Wasatch Range about 40 miles north of Salt Lake City. Housing ranges from historic downtown and east-bench neighborhoods to newer subdivisions and apartments. Landmarks include Historic 25th Street, Ogden Union Station, Peery's Egyptian Theater, and Weber State University.

  • Weber School District, countywide: 4,000–4,999 employees
  • Weber State University, Ogden: 3,000–3,999 employees
  • McKay-Dee Hospital (Intermountain Health), Ogden: Regional hospital

  • Williams International, Ogden: Jet engines; announced 316 new jobs in 2025
  • America First Credit Union, Ogden area: Credit union headquarters
  • Fresenius, Ogden: Medical products manufacturing

Winter
  • Three ski areas within a short drive: Snowbasin, Powder Mountain and Nordic Valley.
  • January averages 38°F highs and 22°F lows, a few degrees milder than Cache Valley.
  • Weber County is in the state's winter no-burn program, usually Nov. 1–Mar. 1.
Spring
  • April averages 62°F highs and 39°F lows; May is the wettest month.
  • FrontRunner commuter rail runs from Ogden and Roy to Salt Lake City and Provo year-round, Monday through Saturday.
Summer
  • Hot, dry summers: July averages 92°F highs.
  • Ogden Pioneer Days rodeo week in late July (July 18–24 in 2026).
  • Snowbasin's summer season runs from Father's Day weekend to early October, with about 25 miles of trails.
Fall
  • The Ogden Canyon and Pineview loop is a favorite fall-color drive, peaking late September to early October.
