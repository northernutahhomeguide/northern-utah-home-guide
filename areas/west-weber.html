Plain City

Population ≈ 7,800 (2020 Census)Weber School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Plain City is in northwest Weber County. It was settled in 1859, incorporated in 1944, and has agricultural roots. Population grew about 43% between 2010 and 2020, adding new subdivisions alongside farmland and larger lots. Residents helped found several national trucking companies, including C.R. England, Swift and Knight.

  • The Ogden Canyon and Pineview loop is a favorite fall-color drive, peaking late September to early October.

Farr West

Population ≈ 7,700 (2020 Census)Weber School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Farr West is on the northern edge of Weber County, bordered by Plain City, Willard, Pleasant View, Harrisville and Marriott-Slaterville. Settled in 1858 and incorporated in 1981, it mixes established larger lots with newer subdivisions, and Silver Ridge Elementary opened in 2020.

Marriott-Slaterville

Population ≈ 2,100 (2020 Census)Weber School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Marriott-Slaterville is a small city of about 7.4 square miles just west of Ogden. It was incorporated in 1999 by merging the communities of Marriott and Slaterville, which pioneer families settled in 1852. Much of the city is open and agricultural land, with some newer development.
