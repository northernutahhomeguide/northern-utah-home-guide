Living in Wellsville

Population ≈ 4,050 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

Wellsville is a city of about 4,000 at the base of the Wellsville Mountains in southern Cache Valley, laid out in 1856 as Maughan's Fort and one of the valley's oldest settlements. It sits at the Cache Valley end of US-89/91 through Sardine Canyon, the main route toward Brigham City. Landmarks include the American West Heritage Center, and USU's South Farm and Equestrian Center.
