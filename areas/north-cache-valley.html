Richmond

Population ≈ 2,900 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

Richmond is a small city in northern Cache Valley about 13 miles north of Logan along US-91, settled in 1859 and incorporated in 1868. It has a historic Main Street area with 17 buildings on the National Register of Historic Places, including the 1914 Carnegie library, surrounded by agricultural land. A Pepperidge Farm plant operates in town, and a Lee's Marketplace grocery opened in 2023.

Lewiston

Population ≈ 1,900 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

Lewiston is one of Utah's northernmost cities, sitting near the Idaho border at the north end of Cache Valley. Settled in 1870, it covers roughly 26 square miles, much of it agricultural land, so properties range from in-town lots to larger rural parcels. The 1935 Lewiston Community Building is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Amalga

Population ≈ 480 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

Amalga is a small farming town west of Smithfield in central Cache Valley, about 3.6 square miles in area. It was named for the Amalgamated Sugar Company factory built there in 1916-17, and today it is home to a Schreiber Foods cheese plant and many dairy operations. Amalga shares the Smithfield ZIP code (84335) and post office.
