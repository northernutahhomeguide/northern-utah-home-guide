Richmond

Population ≈ 2,900 (2020 Census)Cache County School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Richmond is a small city in northern Cache Valley about 13 miles north of Logan along US-91, settled in 1859 and incorporated in 1868. It has a historic Main Street area with 17 buildings on the National Register of Historic Places, including the 1914 Carnegie library, surrounded by agricultural land. A Pepperidge Farm plant operates in town, and a Lee's Marketplace grocery opened in 2023.

Winter
  • December is the snowiest month, with about 16 inches on average.
  • Inversion season: on still winter days cold air and haze can settle in the valley. Mandatory no-burn days are usually called Nov. 1–Mar. 1; check air.utah.gov or the UtahAir app.
  • Skiing at family-run Beaver Mountain, about 27 miles up Logan Canyon.
  • Wagon and sleigh rides among hundreds of wild elk at Hardware Ranch, 16 miles east of Hyrum (weekends, about December through mid-February).
Spring
  • April averages 57°F highs and 36°F lows, and a few inches of snow still fall.
  • May is the wettest month of the year (about 2.4 inches).
Summer
  • Warm, dry days: July averages 88°F highs and 60°F lows, with about half an inch of rain.
  • Utah Festival Opera & Musical Theatre's summer season at the Ellen Eccles and Utah theatres in Logan.
  • Logan Canyon trails, Tony Grove Lake and day trips over the summit to Bear Lake.
Fall
  • Logan Canyon is a top fall-color drive; color builds from mid-September and peaks in late September to early October.
  • October averages 61°F highs and 39°F lows. Snow usually returns in November.

Before you buy in Richmond: good to know5
  • Culinary water and sewer are provided by Richmond City; garbage is contracted to Econo Waste.
  • Secondary (irrigation) water is provided by Richmond Irrigation & Power Company, a private shareholder-owned mutual company (not the city) — ask whether water shares convey with a property.
  • The irrigation company has imposed watering-day restrictions in dry years.
  • Fire and ambulance service is linked through Smithfield City.
  • Cache County School District reconfigured grades for 2026-27 (K-5 elementary, 6-8 middle, 9-12 high) and adjusted boundaries; confirm school assignment for a specific address with the district's boundary map.

Lewiston

Population ≈ 1,900 (2020 Census)Cache County School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Lewiston is one of Utah's northernmost cities, sitting near the Idaho border at the north end of Cache Valley. Settled in 1870, it covers roughly 26 square miles, much of it agricultural land, so properties range from in-town lots to larger rural parcels. The 1935 Lewiston Community Building is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Cornish is a small agricultural town in the northwest corner of Cache Valley near the Idaho border, about 5 square miles in area. It grew up along the Oregon Short Line Railroad and was incorporated in 1937. Housing is mainly homes on rural lots surrounded by farmland.

Amalga is a small farming town west of Smithfield in central Cache Valley, about 3.6 square miles in area. Winter
  • December is the snowiest month, with about 16 inches on average.
  • Inversion season: on still winter days cold air and haze can settle in the valley. Mandatory no-burn days are usually called Nov. 1–Mar. 1; check air.utah.gov or the UtahAir app.
  • Skiing at family-run Beaver Mountain, about 27 miles up Logan Canyon.
  • Wagon and sleigh rides among hundreds of wild elk at Hardware Ranch, 16 miles east of Hyrum (weekends, about December through mid-February).
Spring
  • April averages 57°F highs and 36°F lows, and a few inches of snow still fall.
  • May is the wettest month of the year (about 2.4 inches).
Summer
  • Warm, dry days: July averages 88°F highs and 60°F lows, with about half an inch of rain.
  • Utah Festival Opera & Musical Theatre's summer season at the Ellen Eccles and Utah theatres in Logan.
  • Logan Canyon trails, Tony Grove Lake and day trips over the summit to Bear Lake.
Fall
  • Logan Canyon is a top fall-color drive; color builds from mid-September and peaks in late September to early October.
  • October averages 61°F highs and 39°F lows. Snow usually returns in November.
