Living in Riverdale

Population ≈ 9,343 (2020 Census)Weber School District

Riverdale is a city of about 9,300 residents covering roughly 4.6 square miles along the Weber River, between Ogden and Hill Air Force Base. I-15, I-84 and Riverdale Road run through the city, which is a major regional retail corridor. The Hill Aerospace Museum and the Motor-Vu Drive-in are within city limits.

Good to know before you buy in Riverdale4
  • Direct access to both I-15 and I-84; Riverdale Road is a high-traffic commercial corridor.
  • Hill AFB lies to the south and Ogden-Hinckley Airport to the northwest; a major rail line runs through the city.
  • Weber Basin Water notified Riverdale of a 20% water supply restriction — check current watering rules.
  • Roy Water Conservancy District provides secondary water to a small portion of Riverdale.
