Living in Harrisville

Population ≈ 7,000 (2020 Census)Weber School District

Harrisville is a small city of about 3 square miles just northwest of Ogden along Highway 89. Settled in 1850 and named for pioneer Martin H. Harris, it combines older homes with newer subdivisions and townhome developments.
