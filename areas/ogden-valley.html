Huntsville

Population ≈ 573 (2020 Census)Weber School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Huntsville is a 0.7-square-mile incorporated town in Ogden Valley at about 4,900 feet, roughly twelve miles east of Ogden and next to Pineview Reservoir. It is home to the Shooting Star Saloon (opened 1879) and the Huntsville History Center. Housing is mostly single-family homes on established town lots, with resort and mountain properties in the surrounding valley.

Winter
  • Deep snow: about 108 inches a year, with nearly 29 inches in January alone, and January lows near 10°F.
  • Snowbasin, Powder Mountain and Nordic Valley are all in the valley.
  • Ice fishing on Pineview Reservoir, mainly for perch and crappie.
  • SR-158 (Eden to Powder Mountain) and SR-226 (Snowbasin) are on UDOT's winter traction-law list.
Spring
  • Snow lingers: April still averages about 5 inches, with highs near 56°F.
  • Tiger musky fishing at Pineview picks up as the water warms, roughly May to July (catch and release).
Summer
  • Mild summers: July averages 87°F highs and cool 50°F nights.
  • Boating, beaches and camping at Pineview Reservoir (Anderson Cove, Jefferson Hunt).
Fall
  • Fall color around the Pineview loop peaks late September to early October.
  • October nights drop near freezing, and November averages about a foot of snow.

Before you buy in Huntsville: good to know5
  • Town operates both culinary and secondary water systems, with metering and a water conservation plan.
  • Huntsville remains its own town, surrounded by the newly incorporated Ogden Valley City (certificate issued January 2, 2026).
  • Ogden Canyon (SR-39) and Trappers Loop (SR-167) are the main winter routes to the Wasatch Front.
  • Town offices have limited hours (Mon & Wed 9 a.m.-1 p.m.); municipal code and zoning map are online.
  • Seasonal parking passes and burn permits are administered by the town.

Eden

Population ≈ 690 (2020 Census, CDP)Weber School District

Eden lies in Ogden Valley between the North and Middle Forks of the Ogden River, just north of Pineview Reservoir. Historically an unincorporated community, the Eden area is now within Ogden Valley City, incorporated in January 2026. Housing includes rural acreage, mountain homes, and resort-area properties near Wolf Creek, Nordic Valley, and Powder Mountain.

Before you buy in Eden: good to know4
  • Now part of Ogden Valley City (incorporated January 2, 2026) — zoning, permits, and short-term rental rules are transitioning from Weber County; verify which jurisdiction governs a property.
  • Many Ogden Valley homes rely on private or community wells; confirm water rights, shares, and well approvals before buying or building.
  • Short-term rentals require a short-term rental license and business license; HOA/CC&R restrictions still apply.
  • Mountain roads via North Ogden Divide, Ogden Canyon, and Trappers Loop can be affected by winter weather.
