Brigham City

Population ≈ 19,650 (2020 Census)Box Elder School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsEventsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Brigham City is the county seat of Box Elder County, set at the base of the Wasatch Range at the northern end of the Wasatch Front on US-89/91 and I-15. Housing ranges from historic homes near the downtown Main Street corridor to newer subdivisions on the city's edges. Notable sites include the historic Hotel Brigham, the Brigham City Museum-Gallery, and the 1928 "Gateway to the World's Greatest Wild Bird Refuge" arch over Main Street.

Winter
  • The least snowy area in this guide: about 38 inches a year in Brigham City.
  • Willard Bay State Park is open all year, with winter bald-eagle viewing and ice fishing.
  • Box Elder County is in the state's winter no-burn program, usually Nov. 1–Mar. 1.
Spring
  • April averages 62°F highs and 36°F lows; May is the wettest month.
  • May 10 marks the anniversary of the Golden Spike at Golden Spike National Historical Park near Promontory.
Summer
  • Fruit Way (old Highway 89 through Brigham City, Perry and Willard): cherries into early August and peaches from late July.
  • Boating, swimming and fishing at Willard Bay; the South Marina is open April through October.
  • Hot summers: July averages 91°F highs.
Fall
  • Brigham City Peach Days downtown in September (Sept. 11–12 in 2026).
  • Fruit stands sell late peaches into early October, and apples and squash into November.

Perry

Population ≈ 5,555 (2020 Census)Box Elder School District
SchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesJobsSeasonsGood to knowCommute

Perry sits directly south of Brigham City between the Wasatch foothills and the Bear River bottoms along US-89, near the mouth of Sardine Canyon. Originally called Three Mile Creek, it has shifted from orchards and farmland to a mix of established homes and newer subdivisions. The Heritage Theatre, housed in a former meeting house, presents live productions.
