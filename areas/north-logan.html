Living in North Logan

Population ≈ 11,000 (2020 Census)Cache County School District

North Logan sits directly north of Logan on the east side of Cache Valley, with views of the Wellsville Mountains and the Mt. Naomi Wilderness. Settled in 1890 and incorporated in 1934, it is mainly made up of residential subdivisions. It is home to Green Canyon High School, Cache Valley Hospital and Elk Ridge Park, site of the annual Pumpkin Walk.
