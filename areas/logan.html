Living in Logan

Population ≈ 52,800 (2020 Census)Logan City School District
What's newSchoolsLakes & trailsEat & shopUtilitiesEventsGood to knowCommute

Logan is the Cache County seat and the largest city in Cache Valley, covering about 18.5 square miles at the mouth of Logan Canyon. It is home to the main campus of Utah State University. Landmarks include the 1883 Cache County Historic Courthouse, the Ellen Eccles Theatre and Zootah at Willow Park.

Before you buy in Logan: good to know4
  • Logan City provides its own electric (Logan Light & Power), water, sewer, and trash/recycling service.
  • Connect Transit runs a zero-fare bus system in the area.
  • Some areas within Logan are served by Cache County School District rather than Logan City School District; confirm by address.
  • Utah State University sets much of the local rhythm — expect higher rental demand and traffic near campus during the school year.
