How buying works in Utah

  1. Plan togetherWe talk through your budget, towns and timeline, and sign a buyer agreement that spells out how I'm paid.
  2. Get pre-approvedA local lender's pre-approval makes your offer stronger and sets your real price range.
  3. Tour homesI send listings that match your checklist, and we tour the ones worth seeing in person.
  4. Write the offerUtah uses a Real Estate Purchase Contract (REPC) with set deadlines for disclosures, due diligence, financing and settlement.
  5. Due diligenceHome inspection, radon test, sewer scope, water share and survey checks, and repair negotiation.
  6. Appraisal and loan approvalThe lender orders the appraisal and finalizes your loan.
  7. Settlement and keysYou sign at the title company, and the home is yours once the deed records.
