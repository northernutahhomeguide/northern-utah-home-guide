Each calculator opens with example figures. Change any box and the results update as you type. These are estimates; I'll run exact numbers with you for your situation.

Estimated cash to you at settlement

Utah has no real estate transfer tax. Commission is negotiable and set in your listing agreement. Buyer-agent compensation is optional and negotiated with each offer.

Estimated monthly payment

Utah's primary-residence exemption taxes 55% of a home's value, so property tax varies widely by town. Check the county assessor for a specific address.

Approximate price range

Lenders look at total monthly debt as a share of gross income. A local lender's pre-approval is the real number; I'm happy to connect you with one.

After years, buying leaves you

Compares total rent paid against the cost of owning (interest, upkeep, taxes, insurance and about 7% to sell), minus the equity you build. Down payment is counted as your money either way.

Monthly cash flow

Investment properties are taxed at full value in Utah (no primary-residence exemption), so use a non-owner-occupied tax estimate. Short-term rental rules vary by city.
