Ways to help your home sell

How a sale unfolds

  1. Walk-through and pricingWe tour the home together, review recent sales nearby and set a price and launch date.
  2. Prep weekSmall repairs, cleaning and staging, then professional photos once the home is ready.
  3. Live on the MLSYour listing goes out across the MLS and major home search sites, with showings scheduled through one system.
  4. Offers and negotiationWe compare offers on price, financing, concessions and deadlines, not just the top number.
  5. Due diligenceThe buyer's inspection, radon test and any repair requests, all on the REPC timeline.
  6. Appraisal and financingThe lender's appraisal and loan approval, with a plan ready if the value comes in low.
  7. SettlementYou sign at the title company, and funds are sent once the deed records.
