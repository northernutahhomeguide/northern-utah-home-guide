Things that work differently here

  • No Prop 13. Utah homes are assessed at market value every year, but a primary residence is taxed on only 55% of that value, and rates are much lower.
  • Water shares. Many homes have separate secondary (irrigation) water, and water rights don't automatically come with a property. Read what to ask.
  • Real winters. Logan averages about 62 inches of snow a year; Ogden Valley over 100. Snow-rated tires matter on canyon roads.
  • Winter inversions. Some calm winter days bring haze in the valleys. How locals handle it.
  • Groceries are taxed at a reduced 3% rate in Utah; California exempts most groceries.

Staying close to family in California

  • As of early 2026, Breeze Airways flew nonstop between Ogden and Orange County (John Wayne). Source
  • Salt Lake City International, the region's main airport, is about 89 miles (roughly 1½ hours) from Logan and closer from Ogden. Source
  • Driving? I-15 runs the whole way from Southern California to Ogden, and US-89/91 continues through Sardine Canyon to Cache Valley.
